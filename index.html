IOVIX Privacy Policy

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information the IOVIX app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It covers IOVIX's account system, learning features, the IVANSHY AI assistant, the code compiler, coins and subscriptions, and advertising. It does not cover any third-party app, website, or service you may reach from within IOVIX.

Information You Provide

• Account sign-in: when you create an account or sign in, IOVIX uses Firebase Authentication with either an email and password or Google Sign-In. Depending on the method you choose, this may give IOVIX your email address and the name/profile info your Google account shares.

Information Collected Automatically

• Account identifier: your Firebase Authentication user ID (uid), used to link your progress, coins, and entitlements to your account on our backend.

How We Use Information

We use the information above to: operate your account and keep you signed in; save and restore your course progress, coins, and streaks; verify your Premium subscription and unlock the features it includes; enforce fair-use limits on the IVANSHY AI assistant; run code you submit to the compiler; show a leaderboard; and provide customer support if you contact us. We do not sell your information.

IVANSHY AI Assistant

When you type or speak a question to IVANSHY, the text of your prompt is sent — over an authenticated connection tied to your account — to the IOVIX backend, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply. The reply is returned to your device; IOVIX does not store your prompts or IVANSHY's responses beyond what is needed to process that single request. The backend does record how many AI requests your account has made today, purely to apply the free-tier daily limit (Premium accounts are not capped by this mechanism).

Compiler and Code Execution

The compiler and practice features are Premium features. When you run code, the source code you submitted (and any input you provided) is sent from your device to the IOVIX backend, which forwards it to a third-party code-execution provider to compile and run it; the output is returned to your device. IOVIX does not permanently store your submitted source code beyond what is needed to process that execution request.

Course Progress and Learning Data

Your course progress is reflected through the coins, streaks, quiz-attempt records, and course-unlock records described above, all stored in Firestore under your account so they are available across sessions and devices signed in to the same account.

Coins, Purchases and Subscriptions

Payments for Premium subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play Billing — IOVIX never sees or stores your payment card details. After a purchase, the app sends Google Play's purchase token to the IOVIX backend, which verifies it directly with Google Play. The backend stores a one-way hash of that token (not the token itself) to prevent the same purchase from being linked to more than one account, along with your current subscription/entitlement state. Coins earned or spent in the app are an in-app feature, not real currency, and are tracked as part of your account's economy data described above. Subscription cancellation and payment management are handled entirely inside Google Play, which the app links to directly.

Advertising and AdMob

IOVIX uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show banner and interstitial ads to non-Premium users; Premium subscribers do not see ads. The app currently uses Google's public test ad units during development; production ad units will be enabled closer to launch. When ads are served, Google's advertising systems may process information such as device and app information and, where applicable and permitted by your consent choices, advertising identifiers, in order to select and measure ads. IOVIX itself does not receive or store this advertising data — it is processed by Google under its own policies.

Google UMP / Consent

Where required (for example, in the EU/UK and other regions Google's consent policy covers), IOVIX uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show a consent form before requesting personalized ads. If you do not consent, or no consent message is required in your region, ads are still shown in Google's non-personalized mode. You can review or change your consent choice again the next time UMP determines it needs to re-prompt you, per Google's own UMP behavior.

Data Storage and Security

Your account and progress data is stored using Firebase's infrastructure. Backend endpoints that read or write your data require a valid, signed-in Firebase session — requests are not accepted from unauthenticated callers. Purchase tokens are stored as one-way hashes rather than in plaintext. We use these and other reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed 100% secure.

Data Retention

We retain your account, progress, coin/economy, and subscription data for as long as your account remains active, so that your progress and entitlements are preserved. IVANSHY prompts and compiler source code are used only to process the single request they were sent for and are not separately retained by IOVIX afterward. Where your information passes through a third-party service (Google Gemini, the code-execution provider, Google Play, or Google's advertising systems), any retention by that service is governed by its own policy, linked above, which IOVIX does not control.

Account Deletion / Data Deletion

IOVIX does not yet offer in-app self-service account deletion — this is a planned feature. You can sign out of your account at any time from Settings. Until in-app deletion is available, you can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at iovixsupport@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.

Children's Privacy

IOVIX is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at iovixsupport@gmail.com so we can address it.
